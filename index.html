// about_me

Dear World,

I'd like to formally introduce myself as the web designer you didn't know you needed. Now that you've found me, I suspect you'll wonder how you ever managed without me.

My name is Richard Nimoh. I'm based in Ghana — yes, I know. You probably had your cursor hovering over someone in London or Berlin, because apparently a European timezone is a prerequisite for knowing what good design looks like. I'm here to respectfully, professionally, and with great affection — prove that wrong.

I build with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Mid? No. There's something timeless about doing the fundamentals properly, and besides, I'm already on my way to expanding my stack. Watch this space.

I take what I do seriously enough to do it properly, but not so seriously that I've forgotten how to be human about it.

I've delivered for real clients. One of them, from Upwork, liked what I built so much they came back for a second project — which I choose to interpret as the digital equivalent of a standing ovation. I'm currently finishing an ecommerce site that has been equally challenging and deeply satisfying, the way the best things usually are.

I won't pretend I have ten years and a trophy shelf to boast about. What I have is a genuine commitment to every project I touch, a habit of communicating before problems become surprises, and a track record — small but spotless — of shipping work that makes clients happy.

So if you have a business, I believe it has a spot reserved for it online — a place where it can exist the way it deserves to exist in the world. And I'd very much like to be the one who builds that for you.

The world is full of web designers. But you're here, still reading this. I think that means something.

Warmly, professionally, and with great enthusiasm —

Richard Nimoh

Web Designer. Reliable Artist. Building for you.
